MeshLive connects nearby phones directly over encrypted Bluetooth, with no internet, SIM card, account or server. It is made for blackouts, disasters, censorship, remote places and crowded events.

<b>What you can do</b>
<ul>
<li>Call people nearby: voice or video, end-to-end encrypted from phone to phone.</li>
<li>Go live: share your camera with everyone around you, or go audio only, like a local radio that keeps playing with the screen off.</li>
<li>Reach further: phones in between pass calls and streams along, beyond your own Bluetooth range (about 10 to 30 metres per phone).</li>
<li>Stay private: your broadcast ID is random and changes every 15 minutes; strangers see a random name; your name, if you set one, is never broadcast.</li>
<li>No tracking, no ads, no analytics.</li>
</ul>

<b>Permissions</b>
Nearby devices (to find and connect to phones), Camera and Microphone (for calls and going live), Notifications and foreground services (so calls ring and streams keep going with the screen off). An optional "Share app nearby" screen uses a local Wi-Fi Direct link, which is why the internet and Wi-Fi state permissions are declared; the app never contacts the internet for calls or streams.

<b>Status</b>
Beta. Quality and range depend on the phones and their Bluetooth, and some phone pairs still struggle; the project tracks them openly. Please do not rely on it where your safety depends on it.
