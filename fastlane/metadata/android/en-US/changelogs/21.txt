0.2.0-beta: privacy is on by default.
- Rotating discovery IDs: a scanner nearby can no longer follow your phone by one fixed ID.
- Hidden identities: calls hide both phones' identity keys from a Bluetooth sniffer.
- Strangers see a throwaway identity; your real one goes to verified contacts and to people whose call you accept.
- Older app versions cannot see or call updated phones. Switches are in Settings.
- Calls to a phone that refuses the old handshake fail within seconds, with a reason.
